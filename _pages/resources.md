  <summary>Academia</summary>
  <div class="resource-content">
    <p>As a first-generation immigrant, I often found myself learning academia’s unspoken rules on the fly. Along the way, I've come across resources — some stumbled upon and others kindly shared by mentors and friends — that made my journey a little easier. I’m sharing them here in the hope that they offer someone else the kind of guidance and encouragement I’ve been lucky to receive.</p>

    <p><em>Note</em>: my background is in psychology and statistics, so these may not be universally applicable.</p>

    <h3>Applying to grad school</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><a href="https://github.com/sdhutchins/grad-school-app-toolkit">Grad school application toolkit</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/0kckzfj7fkw09nsnh6vuk/zaki_gradApp_guide.pdf?rlkey=cdlctjuzx0ir9nivsvowbobra&e=2&dl=0">Jamil Zaki's guide to applying for a PhD in psychology</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://lucylai.com/blog/gradapps">Lucy Lai's PhD application guide</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://www.adaniabutto.com/resources/gradschool">Adani Abutto's collection of resources</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~aramdas/checklists.html">Aaditya Ramdas' Stat-ML PhD checklist</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/gradadmissions/comments/a4vpc4/master_list_of_interview_questions/">Master list of interview questions</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://www.eugenevinitsky.com/posts/coldemails/">A guide to cold emailing</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://www.queerinai.com/grad-app-aid">Grad app aid</a></li>
      <li>The best interview advice from my undergrad advisor <a href="https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/psychology/directory/core-training-faculty/manke-miller-kody.html">Kody Manke-Miller</a>: <strong>show that you’re a curious &amp; interesting person</strong></li>
    </ul>

    <h3>Other</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><a href="https://karpathy.github.io/2016/09/07/phd/">Andrej Karpathy's survival guide to a PhD</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://github.com/joshuakalla/talks_and_advice/blob/master/advice/Dahis_Advice_Research.pdf">Advice for academic research</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://puddle-wealth-097.notion.site/Post-Bac-b6452ddc092f48b2b85de5fac19ac831">Post-bac programs</a></li>
      <li><a href="https://phdcomics.com/comics/archive.php?comicid=47">PHD Comics</a></li>
    </ul>

    <p>I'm always happy to talk with undergraduates and early-career researchers about graduate school, research, and my own academic experiences. I by no means have it all figured out, but I'm glad to share any of my materials if they would be useful. Don't hesitate to <a href="mailto:amorai@mit.edu">reach out via email</a>.</p>
